# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** November 3, 2026

**Chronos** is designed with privacy as a foundational principle.

### 1. Data Collection
Chronos does not collect, track, transmit, or store any personal information or telemetry on any remote servers.

### 2. Local Storage
All app preferences (such as your chosen theme, custom alarm configurations, saved world cities, and Pomodoro routine preferences) are stored strictly on your local device using standard browser `localStorage`. This data never leaves your device.

### 3. Permissions
- **Audio:** Chronos utilizes the Web Audio API to produce local acoustic chimes and ticks. No microphone access or audio recording is requested or performed.
- **Desktop Notifications:** If enabled, notifications are triggered locally on your device to inform you when a timer, alarm, or focus interval concludes.

### 4. Third-Party Services
Chronos operates 100% client-side with full offline support via its Service Worker. No third-party analytical trackers, ads, or cookies are utilized.
